# FI UI specification

## Credit Limit View / Create / Edit Interfaces (6.17–6.19, WU-FI-001c)

Route `/ar/credit-limits` (menu Finance → Customer credit limits, `AR.CREDIT.VIEW`). The BlueWhale Accounting interface numbers 4.4.1.6.1–16 stay reserved for their original collection and payment functions, so credit limits continue from 6.17. Create (6.18) and Edit (6.19) share one dialog layout but are distinct documented states. Edit locks the customer and explains why. The list shows saved values only. Success messages repeat the server-returned customer, period, exact amount, currency and version, and the list is then re-read. A deactivated row explains that it is history only. Errors: 400 marks the fields and focuses the first invalid one; 409 overlap stays in the dialog; a stale 409 or a 422 not-active closes the dialog, explains the reason above the list and reloads it; 5xx shows the trace ID; network loss offers a retry. Customer code/name come from the FI response via the MD batch label contract, including inactive and soft-deleted history; the picker cache is a fallback and a missing label displays a translated Customer #id (ADR-077). Screenshots: `tests/qa/FI/screenshots/6.17-*` (26, three languages, light/dark).

## Planned interfaces

WU-FI-000d creates no page and allocates no REQ_IO_A ID. WU-FI-001c defined the actual Credit Limit View/Create/Edit interfaces, fields, three-language strings, disabled reasons, empty/error states and light/dark screenshots after the API is working. A preview must display its evaluation date and advisory status without a save-success message. The BlueWhale Accounting interface numbers 4.4.1.6.1–16 remain reserved for their original collection and payment functions.

WU-FI-002d adds no UI or REQ_IO_A ID. The future Sales Receivable Source View Interface will show immutable SD source identity, original/open receivable, base/document currency totals, journal reference and linked credit allocations with company scope. Zero-change replay is a warning, and missing FI posting is never displayed as Posted. Its actual interface number, fields, translations, permission states and screenshots belong to WU-FI-002c after the read API is available; SD invoice/return screens remain SD-owned.

WU-FI-003d adds no UI or REQ_IO_A ID. A future Purchase Payable Source View Interface will display the immutable PUR invoice, supplier, saved original/base amount components, AP original/open balance and GL journal reference. It must distinguish missing, failed, committed and unchanged replay without a false success toast; access and empty/error states use three languages and light/dark themes. The actual interface number, fields, translations and screenshots belong to WU-FI-003c. The matching and POST screen remains PUR-owned.

WU-FI-004d adds no page or REQ_IO_A ID. The planned Journal View Interface shows immutable source, period, account snapshots, debit and credit totals, with company and permission scope. The planned Accounting Period View/Edit Interface shows inclusive dates, Open/Closed state, version and why a period cannot be changed. Three-language light/dark states must cover normal, empty, search-empty, denied, validation, success, unchanged replay, server error and timeout. Actual interface numbers, fields, translations and screenshots belong to WU-FI-004c after the SQL/HTTP contract is working; upstream SD/PUR screens own their posting actions.

WU-FI-005d reserves the baseline Sales Collection View/Create/Edit Interfaces (4.4.1.6.1–3) and Collection Write-off View/Create Interfaces (4.4.1.6.13–14); edit may share create layout but remains a distinct documented state. Receipt pages show company/customer, business date, bank reference, currency, original/allocated/unapplied amount, state and source/journal link. Settlement pages show one receipt, ordered invoice allocations, each open balance, remaining unapplied total and per-row validation reason. Approve is unavailable until SYS permits it; disabled actions explain state/permission. Three-language light/dark screens distinguish normal, empty, search-empty, validation, 403/404/409/422/500, timeout, committed success and unchanged replay. A receipt approval must say unapplied cash was posted; it must not claim an invoice was paid. Actual REQ_IO_A IDs, UI fields and screenshots belong to WU-FI-005c after working SQL/HTTP. This design unit adds no page.

WU-FI-006d reserves Purchase Payment View/Create/Edit Interfaces (4.4.1.6.7–9) and Payment Write-off View/Create Interfaces (4.4.1.6.15–16); edit is a distinct documented state. Payment pages show company/supplier, saved date, bank reference, base currency, original/allocated/unapplied amount, state and journal link. Settlement pages show one Posted payment, ordered FI payable allocations, each current open balance, remaining unapplied payment and row-specific rejection reason. A payment approval says unapplied payment was posted and does not claim AP was settled. A zero-change replay is a warning, while a committed settlement displays the actual decrease in open AP. Disabled actions explain state or permission. Three-language light/dark normal, empty, search-empty, validation, 403/404/409/422/500, timeout and partial-use states must match fresh saved data. REQ_IO_A IDs, actual fields and screenshots belong to WU-FI-006c after SQL/HTTP implementation. This design unit adds no page.

WU-FI-007d reserves Other Collection View/Create/Edit Interfaces (4.4.1.6.4–6). The planned list/detail shows income category, payer description, bank/reference, state, saved base amount and journal, with a visible “non-AR income” label. The form accepts only FI-owned Draft fields; it offers no invoice selector or client account override. Pending/Posted controls explain their disabled state. Successful final approval says income was posted; unchanged replay warns of zero change; timeout requests a fresh read before any claim of success. Empty, search-empty, denied, field validation, server error and traceId states require three languages and light/dark review. Actual field-level REQ_IO_A IDs, translations and screenshots belong to WU-FI-007c after SQL/HTTP implementation. No page is delivered here.

WU-FI-008d reserves Other Payment View/Create/Edit Interfaces (4.4.1.6.10–12). The planned list/detail shows expense category, payee description, bank/reference, state, saved base amount and journal with a clear “non-AP expense” label. The form has no purchase-invoice selector or client account override. Pending/Posted actions explain their disabled state. Final approval says expense was posted and cash paid, without claiming an AP invoice was settled; unchanged replay warns of zero change, and timeout requires a fresh read. Empty, search-empty, denied, validation, server error and traceId states need three languages and light/dark review. Actual field-level REQ_IO_A IDs, translations and screenshots belong to WU-FI-008c after SQL/HTTP implementation. No page is delivered here.

WU-FI-009d adds no FI page or REQ_IO_A ID. BlueWhale Client/Supplier Statement Interfaces 4.4.1.7.20–21 remain BI-owned. They must show partner, company, base currency, inclusive date range, committed cutoff, opening, dated movement lines with immutable source link and running balance, closing and empty-period explanation. Page navigation, print and export retain the same cutoff; an expired cutoff asks for refresh, and a new backdated posting appears only after refresh. Three-language light/dark normal, empty, search-empty, denied, invalid range, 500 with traceId and timeout states belong to WU-FI-009b with BI interface IDs and screenshots. A read never shows a posting success message.

WU-FI-010d adds no FI page or REQ_IO_A ID. BlueWhale Cash & Bank Report Interface 4.4.1.7.17 remains BI-owned. The later page must label the selected MD cash/bank account, company base currency, date range, committed cutoff, opening, debit/credit movements, running and closing **book** balances, and immutable FI source/journal links. Export and page navigation retain the same cutoff. It must never label book balance as bank statement balance or imply that an unmatched external row has posted. Expired cutoff, empty period, hidden account, 400/409/422/500 with traceId and timeout have specific translated messages in three languages and both themes; a read shows no posting success. Actual interface IDs, screenshot QA and data assertions belong to WU-FI-010b. External matching UI belongs to later reconciliation work.

WU-FI-011d plans Bank Statement Import, Reconciliation Match and Reconciliation Review Interfaces under FI/CM; actual interface numbering and `REQ_IO_A` fields belong to WU-FI-011c after SQL/HTTP work. Import displays exact imported/skipped/rejected counts and row-specific causes; partial or zero-change result is a warning. Match view labels bank and **book** sides separately, with each side's date, signed amount, currency, identity, opening/closing and FI cutoff. Ambiguous candidates remain visible with reason. Reviewer sees importer/matcher, frozen evidence digest, dispositions and differences; Approve is disabled with a reason when arithmetic, cutoff, identity or reviewer separation fails. Approval says only that reconciliation was recorded, never that GL was adjusted. Three-language light/dark QA covers normal, empty, all-skipped, partial, all-rejected, malformed file, 403/404/409/422/500 with traceId, timeout and unknown commit; E2E compares displayed counts/amounts to fresh saved FI evidence and cash-book reads. This unit creates no page.
WU-FI-012d plans Fixed Asset View/Create/Edit, Depreciation Preview/Run and Disposal View/Create Interfaces. Card detail labels cost, committed depreciation and calculated net book value separately from linked Posted GL journals. Preview is explicitly unposted; a run shows exact posted/skipped/failed counts with each asset reason and warns for partial or zero change. Disposal preview shows proceeds, remaining book value and gain/loss before approval, and approval cannot imply a cash receipt unless its own source posted. Disabled actions explain Draft/Pending/Posted/Disposed, closed period or permission. Three-language light/dark QA covers normal, empty, search-empty, field errors, replay, partial results, 403/404/409/422/500 with traceId and unknown timeout; E2E compares screen figures and counts with saved FI sources and journals. Actual interface numbers, `REQ_IO_A` fields and screenshots belong to WU-FI-012e after SQL/HTTP implementation. No page is delivered here.

WU-FI-013d plans Tax Return View/Prepare, Reconciliation Review and Filing Evidence Interfaces. A draft visibly says Not Filed and shows company, regime, policy version, period, committed cutoff, output, eligible input, adjustments, net payable/credit and source count. Each difference shows source and Posted GL links, account/period, expected/actual and reason; an unexplained difference disables Approve with a reason. Internal approval cannot claim government filing. External reference/date remain distinct from reviewer approval. A backdated source prompts a new revision, never silently changes an approved one. Zero/partial/incomplete outcomes warn with exact counts and per-source reasons; timeout is unknown pending fresh read. Three-language light/dark QA covers normal, no sources, search-empty, denied, validation, 409/422/500 with traceId and network interruption. E2E compares screen amounts and counts to saved FI detail and Posted GL at the same cutoff. Actual interface IDs, REQ_IO_A fields and screenshots belong to WU-FI-013c. No page is delivered here.

WU-FI-014d plans FX Exposure, Settlement Difference and Period Valuation Interfaces. They show original/base currency, historical and settlement rate snapshots, original amount, historical/valued base, realised/unrealised difference, remaining exposure, period/cutoff and source/journal links. Preview and unposted draft are visibly distinct from Posted. Zero, partial, all-skipped and unchanged outcomes need exact counts and reasons with warning severity; timeout remains unresolved until committed-source lookup. Three-language light/dark QA covers normal, empty, search-empty, denied, stale rate/version, validation and 500 with traceId. E2E compares screen amounts, counts and status to saved FI sources and Posted GL at one cutoff. Actual interface IDs and REQ_IO_A fields belong to WU-FI-014c. No page is delivered here.

WU-FI-015d plans Accounting Period View/Close/Reopen, Fiscal Year View/Close/Reopen, and Adjustment View/Create Interfaces. The readiness panel labels its committed cutoff, policy, per-family complete/pending/blocked counts, GL/subledger differences, named blockers and waiver evidence. Close is disabled with the exact missing permission, stale revision or blocker reason; it never calls a preview “Closed.” Reopen shows the required reverse order, second reviewer, downstream report/tax dependency and compensation source. Year close shows retained-earnings calculation and next-year carry-forward separately from current-year Posted balances. Zero-change replay, empty period and incomplete or partial readiness use warning severity and exact counts. Three-language light/dark QA covers normal, empty, search-empty, validation, 403/404/409/422/500 with traceId, timeout and unknown commit. E2E compares screen status, counts and journal IDs to fresh FI reads at the same cutoff. Actual interface numbers, `REQ_IO_A` IDs, translations and screenshots belong to WU-FI-015c after real SQL/HTTP implementation. No page is delivered here.
