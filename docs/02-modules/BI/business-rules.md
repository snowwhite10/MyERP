# BI reporting business rules

## WU-BI-000d financial reports (design only)

- BR-BI-001: BI composes report presentation from owner module Application/Contracts reads only. FI owns AR/AP, GL, cash book, statement and other income/expense accounting facts. BI stores no accounting balance and has no EF navigation or SQL join to `ar_`, `ap_`, `gl_`, `cm_`, `sal_`, `pur_` or `md_` tables.
- BR-BI-002: Every request fixes one authorised company, relevant partner or fund account, inclusive business-date range, base currency and committed cutoff. The owner contract enforces its own company and SYS data scope; BI's VIEW/EXPORT permission never broadens it. BI treats inaccessible identities as 404.
- BR-BI-003: BI preserves FI's saved original/base amounts, posting/source identity and rounding. It does not reconstruct a historic balance from today's MD currency rate, document state or a current open balance. AP/AR detail comes from Posted principal and committed allocations; unapplied money and other income/expense stay separate.
- BR-BI-004: FI creates a durable, committed source-set cutoff, not merely a maximum row ID. BI binds every page and export to its filters and cutoff. A later or backdated posting appears only after a fresh request. Tampered, expired or filter-mismatched tokens fail with 409 or 400 as appropriate; BI never mixes sets.
- BR-BI-005: BI checks decimal totals from FI: statement closing = opening + signed movements; cash closing = opening + debit - credit; AR/AP open = Posted principal plus credits less committed allocations. A mismatch is an explicit incomplete/error state with source IDs, not an invented balancing row. Empty ranges retain opening = closing.
- BR-BI-006: A report is read-only. Preview, pagination, print and export create no journal, allocation, workflow or business audit mutation. Export escapes spreadsheet formula-leading values, retains deterministic order and includes cutoff, filters and currency. Unsupported currency, absent FI source or arithmetic overflow is 422 with named reason.
- BR-BI-007: Empty report, zero rows, incomplete source, expired snapshot and unknown timeout have distinct translated messages. BI never says data were saved by a read. A failed export does not yield a partial downloadable file. Three languages, light/dark mode and explicit loading/denied/error states apply when pages are implemented.
- BR-BI-008: The initial financial slice is base-currency and one company at a time. FX, consolidated companies, scheduled delivery and cross-currency netting require separate source and security contracts; BI must not infer them from existing rows.

The existing FI source designs are BR-FI-067–082, BR-FI-059–066 and BR-FI-001–050. WU-BI-001a and later units will verify these rules against real SQL once FI source implementations exist.

## WU-BI-001d detail and other-source binding (design only)

- BR-BI-009: AR/AP detail uses FI's as-of Posted principal and committed allocation DTO (REQ_F4116.19.1–2), with original, credit, settled and open base totals across the entire frozen set. BI may format or filter authorised FI rows but cannot recompute a balance from SD/PUR documents, a current FI open balance or today's rate. Zero-open historical rows are labelled separately from outstanding amounts.
- BR-BI-010: Other income/expense uses FI's typed Posted OtherCollection/OtherPayment DTO (REQ_F4116.19.3). BI displays income and expense as separate positive totals and net as income less expense; the FI signed line amount remains intact. Category/account labels are saved posting snapshots. AR/AP settlement, unapplied funds and invoice origin never become other income/expense through BI classification.
- BR-BI-011: BI validates its report-specific VIEW/EXPORT permission, then FI validates source permission and SYS scope on every page/export. A revoked role or partner scope denies the next read despite a valid cutoff. BI carries FI cutoff and filters unchanged; malformed/changed token, expired manifest and incomplete source have distinct translated outcomes. BI never caches an authorised row for an actor who no longer has access.

Traceability: BR-BI-009 → BR-FI-124–128 → REQ_F4117.5.2–3; BR-BI-010 → BR-FI-129–130 → REQ_F4117.5.6–7; BR-BI-011 → BR-FI-127,130–132 → all seven financial report requirements. No product behaviour is delivered by this design unit.

## WU-BI-002d operational source boundary (design only)

- BR-BI-012: BI reads PUR, SD, PP and INV only through their Application/Contracts interfaces. The owner applies current company, SYS data scope and source-specific permission on each read; BI applies report VIEW and later EXPORT separately. No BI SQL join, cross-module EF navigation or copied balance table is allowed.
- BR-BI-013: PUR tracking distinguishes saved order, Posted receipt, Posted return and invoice. `openToReceive = ordered - Posted received + Posted returned` is a tracking measure subject to PUR's own cancellation/return semantics, not an authorisation to receive. Detail/summary use immutable source-line IDs and saved currency, amount, item and supplier snapshots. No FI payable is inferred from receipt or invoice state.
- BR-BI-014: SD tracking distinguishes saved order, Posted delivery, Posted return and Posted invoice. Delivered and invoiced amounts are separate; a return has a linked reversal source. Detail/summary preserve SD's saved prices, tax and currency, and do not infer FI AR or credit from SD status. Open quantity follows SD's approved/cancelled rules, never a negative quantity hidden as zero.
- BR-BI-015: PP owns planned, released, requisitioned and completed document facts. INV owns actual Posted issue/inbound movements. PP's future read contract must carry stable requisition/completion line IDs and confirmed INV movement references, with the same committed source-set guarantee. Until PP is implemented, BI reports 7.3.1–5 must be unavailable with a named incomplete-source result; zero rows would be misleading.
- BR-BI-016: INV balance and flow use Posted immutable movements, base-unit six-decimal quantities and one committed source set. Opening is all movements before `from`; interval movements include both dates; closing is opening plus in minus out. Transfers show both source and destination legs; grouping preserves warehouse/location and does not imply valuation. BI may bind to the existing stock-flow read, but its current per-request snapshot is insufficient for cross-page frozen reports and needs a durable owner token.
- BR-BI-017: For every operational report, owner manifests identify the complete committed eligible source set and bind company, actor scope, filters, sort and cutoff. A maximum ID or wall-clock alone does not prove commit order. Later backdated commits and changed role/scope do not leak into old pages. The owner either returns the same source set, or rejects expired/inconsistent continuation with a named 409/422; BI never silently refreshes.
- BR-BI-018: Summary totals span the full authorised set, not the visible page. Group keys include saved identity, base unit and currency where amounts exist. Mixed currencies are presented separately. Duplicate source links, missing movement provenance, arithmetic overflow or owner totals that disagree with detail cause a named incomplete result; BI does not add a balancing row or silently drop bad rows.
- BR-BI-019: Read, page, print and future export create no business mutation. Empty authorised sets are distinct from unavailable source, all-excluded, denied and timeout. Historical soft-deleted MD references remain identified by saved snapshots without making them selectable for new transactions. Export, when added, must use the same manifest, safe spreadsheet text and no truncated file.

Traceability: 7.1.1–4 → BR-BI-012–13,017–19; 7.2.1–4 → 012,014,017–19; 7.3.1–5 → 012,015,017–19; 7.4.1–3 → 012,016–19. These are planned contracts, not evidence of implemented PUR/SD/PP/INV source endpoints.

## WU-BI-003d dashboard rules (design only)

- BR-BI-020: BI requests each KPI from its owner Application/Contracts read interface. FI owns Posted revenue and open AR, SD owns first-approved order identity, INV owns Posted stock and safety thresholds. BI applies card VIEW permission; each owner independently rechecks source permission, company and SYS scope. Missing owner/source returns a named unavailable state.
- BR-BI-021: Each owner returns a durable committed, filter-bound source manifest for the current interval and its comparison interval or as-of day. Backdated commits cannot enter an existing result; a new request may see them. Every card labels its own cutoff. Cross-owner cards are not an atomic snapshot.
- BR-BI-022: Revenue is net FI Posted sales invoice revenue less FI Posted sales credit revenue in base currency; tax, receipts and non-sales journals are excluded. FI supplies the saved accounting classification and exact decimal total. Order count uses distinct SD first approval IDs, excluding Draft, Pending, Rejected and Void. Reapproval is not a second order.
- BR-BI-023: Open AR is a point-in-time FI balance, with committed allocations and credit notes applied as of the selected date; unapplied collections stay separate. Low-stock count uses INV item/warehouse keys whose Posted on-hand is below a positive saved safety threshold; missing thresholds are not treated as zero. Neither measure is summed over the interval.
- BR-BI-024: The prior interval has the same number of calendar days and ends the day before `from`; AR and low stock compare as-of balances at the day before `from`. If the prior source is unavailable or its value is zero, percentage change is absent with a reason; no divide-by-zero or false 0% display. Negative revenue and AR credits retain their sign.
- BR-BI-025: One card failing never turns another owner's successful card into zero. Cards identify ready, empty, unavailable, incomplete, denied or expired independently. Cached values are not shown after company, filter, role or scope changes until reauthorised. Reading or exporting a dashboard never changes FI, SD, INV, GL or audit business rows.

Traceability: REQ_F4117.6.1 → BR-BI-020–22,024–25; 6.2 → 020–22,024–25; 6.3–4 → 020–21,023–25.
## WU-BI-004d comparison, drill and schedule boundary (design only)

- BR-BI-026: Comparison is allowed only for one report and owner contract that declares comparable metric semantics. Both periods use the same company, owner scope, filters, group keys, currency and base unit; each has an independent durable committed manifest and visible cutoff. A different owner, unsupported grouping or incompatible unit is a named unavailable result.
- BR-BI-027: For each group, `difference = current - comparison`; percentage is `difference / abs(comparison) * 100` only when comparison is nonzero and both sides are complete. A zero denominator shows an undefined percentage with reason, never infinity or a fabricated zero. Money and quantities retain owner precision and rounding; no cross-currency sum is calculated.
- BR-BI-028: Drill token binds report, company, actor scope, group key, original filters, sort and owner manifest. The owner returns source IDs and detail rows from exactly that set. Full-set detail sum must equal the selected aggregate, including return/reversal signs; pagination cannot silently recompute from current rows. Incomplete or expired manifest invalidates drill.
- BR-BI-029: Every comparison side, drill page and export rechecks current BI action permission, owner source permission and SYS scope. A valid old token or artifact never grants access after revocation. Foreign company or out-of-scope source identity is hidden as 404, and no cached data is served to a newly unauthorised actor.
- BR-BI-030: A schedule definition contains only allowlisted report/filter/group keys, one company, one in-app recipient, local hour/minute, configured time zone, daily/weekly cadence, active flag and version. It stores no credentials or unbounded SQL. Initial policy skips a nonexistent local time and chooses the earlier instant for an ambiguous local time, recording the resolution; a due occurrence key uses schedule ID plus resolved instant.
- BR-BI-031: A worker claims each due occurrence with a durable lease and unique occurrence key, so retry or two workers create at most one completed artifact. It revalidates active schedule, recipient company/role, BI EXPORT and owner VIEW at execution. If any check fails, it records a named failed/skipped run and creates no file. A preview's manifest is never reused by a scheduled run.
- BR-BI-032: A successful run fixes a fresh owner manifest, freezes the complete filtered row set, validates full-set totals, writes a safe artifact atomically and records cutoff, filters, count, totals and file digest. A zero-row complete source may produce an explicitly empty artifact; partial/incomplete source, timeout, overflow or oversized file never yields a downloadable artifact. Storage retention and size limit are implementation configuration with a documented bound.
- BR-BI-033: Artifact download rechecks recipient/company, BI EXPORT and owner source authority, expiry and digest. A schedule pause/delete prevents future occurrences but preserves run history and business audit. Job and artifact metadata are BI-owned; owner facts remain with FI/PUR/SD/PP/INV. Read, compare, drill and export do not mutate business source, GL or workflow rows.
- BR-BI-034: HTTP/UI states distinguish scheduled, running, completed, empty completed, skipped, failed, unavailable, denied, expired and unknown timeout with translated reason and occurrence. No success toast is shown for zero new runs or partial/failed execution. A BI schedule or artifact may not expose owner rows through a notification payload; notification carries only an authorised link.

Traceability: REQ_F4117.7.1 → BR-BI-026–27,029; 7.2 → 028–29; 7.3 → 030–31,033; 7.4 → 031–34; 7.5 → 026–29,032–33. BI-ADV-001–023 are planned tests. No runtime behaviour is delivered here.
