The Quality module (QM) is planned to cover incoming inspection, in-process checkpoints, nonconformance decisions and quality reporting under REQ_F41111. Inspection plans specify effective versions, sampling and acceptance limits. Decisions retain their source lot or operation, measurements, quantities, approver and disposition so that accepted, rejected and pending quantities reconcile.

PUR owns receipt and return outcomes, PP owns work-order operations, and INV owns posted stock movements. QM would request actions through those modules' published contracts and show pending or unavailable outcomes explicitly; an inspection decision alone would not post stock. Reports would use fixed source cutoffs and the viewer's authorised scope. QM currently has a design baseline only. Its tables, endpoints, pages, owner integrations and product tests remain to be implemented.
