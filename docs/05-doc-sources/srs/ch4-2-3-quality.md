MyERP shall preserve accounting correctness and a consistent user experience across modules. Domain and Application unit tests shall each achieve at least 80% line coverage (REQ_NF423.1), and all required tests shall pass before changes are merged into main (REQ_NF423.2). Backend and frontend builds shall have zero warnings; the backend treats warnings as errors and the frontend uses strict compilation (REQ_NF423.6). Coverage and test reports must identify the tested revision.

All interface text shall be available in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English, with zero missing translation keys (REQ_NF423.3). Every page shall support light, dark and follow-system themes. Ordinary text shall have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 under WCAG 2.1 AA (REQ_NF423.4). Language-key tests, browser checks and contrast measurements shall verify these requirements for each completed interface.

Money calculations shall reconcile exactly at the defined currency precision: the document header shall equal the sum of rounded detail lines, and posted journal debits shall equal credits (REQ_NF423.5). Unit and integration tests shall cover rounding boundaries and reject an unbalanced posting. A passing calculation test is evidence for that calculation only; it does not establish the quality of unfinished modules.
