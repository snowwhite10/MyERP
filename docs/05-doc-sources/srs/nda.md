**1. Purpose**

The parties may exchange information solely to evaluate, specify, design, build, test, and support MyERP under a separately agreed project scope. Each party may be a disclosing party or a receiving party.

**2. Confidential information**

Confidential information includes nonpublic business records, requirements, designs, source materials, credentials, security information, and personal data disclosed in any form for the stated purpose. A receiving party must identify information as confidential when its nature or the circumstances of disclosure make that treatment reasonable.

**3. Use and protection**

The receiving party may use confidential information only for the purpose above. It must limit access to personnel and advisers who need the information for that purpose, require them to protect it, and use reasonable safeguards against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. It must promptly notify the disclosing party after discovering unauthorized disclosure and cooperate in containment.

**4. Exclusions**

The obligations do not apply to information the receiving party can demonstrate was publicly available without its breach, already lawfully known to it, independently developed without use of the confidential information, or lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidence.

**5. Required disclosure**

If disclosure is required by law or a valid authority, the receiving party may disclose only what is required. Where legally permitted, it must give the disclosing party prompt notice and reasonable assistance to seek protection.

**6. Return and retention**

Upon written request or completion of the agreed purpose, the receiving party must return or securely destroy confidential information, subject to retention required by law or an agreed backup policy. Retained material remains subject to this agreement for <Confidentiality Period>.

**7. Ownership and other terms**

Disclosure grants no intellectual property license or ownership transfer. The parties must agree separately on project deliverables, data processing, liability, governing law, dispute resolution, the agreement term, and any exceptions. No commercial or service commitment arises from this draft.

**8. Execution**

The parties may execute the final agreement only after completing the placeholders and approving its terms through their authorized representatives. The effective date is <Date>.
