The Accounting module (FI) is planned to cover the general ledger, receivables and collections, payables and payments, customer and supplier statements, fixed assets, cash and bank accounts, reconciliation, tax, exchange differences, and period and year closing. A single FI-owned posting service is the route for balanced journal entries from other modules. Sales invoices and purchase invoice matches provide immutable source evidence; FI owns their posted receivable or payable and related ledger entries. Detailed requirements use REQ_F4116.

Posting must validate an open accounting period and postable accounts, balance debit and credit in the company base currency, and commit the source and journal together. A repeated source with the same saved content returns its original result; changed content conflicts. Collections and payments are distinct from their allocation against open receivables or payables. Period closing must reconcile committed source and ledger totals before freezing the period.

FI currently has requirement, rule and contract design slices. It has no accepted FI transaction tables, posting API, accounting pages or real SQL product verification. Master Data's chart of accounts is available as a separate owner capability; it does not by itself constitute an operational general ledger.
