The Human Resources module (HR) is planned to cover employment records, organisation assignments, rosters and attendance, payroll, leave, recruitment, performance reviews and training under REQ_F41114. Effective dates and event history would preserve earlier assignments and corrections. Payroll runs would freeze eligible employees, approved time and leave, rates and component versions so that gross, deductions and net can be reconciled.

HR employment identity remains distinct from SYS login identity. Sensitive employee information would be limited to authorised roles and company scope. FI would confirm any accounting posting; a calculated or exported payroll run would not be described as posted without that confirmation. The current HR work is a design baseline. HR data tables, services, screens, payroll execution and product acceptance are future work.
