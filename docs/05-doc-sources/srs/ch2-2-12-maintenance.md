The Maintenance module (PM) is planned to manage equipment identity and history, preventive schedules, maintenance orders and spare-part requests under REQ_F41112. A due occurrence would keep its equipment and plan version; repeated generation must identify existing, skipped and failed occurrences with reasons. Orders would record approval, labour, downtime and completion evidence.

PM would verify optional fixed-asset identity through FI and request spare reservations, issues and returns through INV. A request or estimated cost would not be presented as a posted inventory or accounting result before its owner confirms it. Equipment retirement and order closure retain historical records. The present PM deliverable is a requirements and design baseline; no PM runtime feature, migration, interface or operational verification exists yet.
