The Customer Relationship Management module (CRM) is planned to manage leads, opportunities, interactions, campaigns and service tickets under REQ_F41113. Leads and opportunities retain owners and status history. Campaign execution would freeze its audience and record delivered, skipped, failed and pending outcomes with reasons. Service tickets would retain their service policy version and a traceable history of resolution and reopening.

MD remains the owner of customer identity and SD the owner of quotations and orders. A qualified lead or won opportunity would link to a confirmed owner record; neither state by itself creates a customer, sale or revenue. Personal contact visibility, consent and company scope require enforcement before any messaging or history display. CRM currently consists of requirements and design only, with no migration, endpoint, page or accepted customer workflow.
