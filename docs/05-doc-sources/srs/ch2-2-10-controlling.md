The Controlling module (CO) is planned to maintain company-scoped cost and profit centre hierarchies, calculate provisional product costs, approve cost baselines and manage budgets with variance analysis. REQ_F41110 defines these four sub-functions. Effective dates and permanent codes preserve historical assignments, while approved versions make budgets and cost calculations reproducible.

CO would obtain posted actuals from FI and committed material and production facts from INV and PP through their published Application contracts. Each calculation must name its policy version and source cutoff. Missing or unpriced source facts are reported as incomplete or unavailable; CO approval does not change inventory valuation or posted journals. The current CO work is a requirements and design baseline. No CO database tables, services, API, screens or operational results have been delivered or accepted.
