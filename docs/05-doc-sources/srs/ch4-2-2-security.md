MyERP shall protect user sessions, company data and business changes at the API boundary. In non-Development environments, APIs shall be served through HTTPS, HTTP requests shall redirect to HTTPS, and HSTS shall have a maximum age of at least 365 days (REQ_NF422.1). Responses shall carry Content Security Policy, X-Content-Type-Options, X-Frame-Options and Referrer-Policy headers (REQ_NF422.7).

An access token shall expire after 15 minutes. A refresh token shall expire after seven days, rotate after use and become invalid immediately on logout (REQ_NF422.2). Five consecutive failed logins shall lock the account for 15 minutes (REQ_NF422.3). Login, registration and password recovery shall each be limited to five requests per IP per minute; ordinary APIs shall be limited to 300 requests per user per minute and exports to ten. A request over a limit shall receive HTTP 429 (REQ_NF422.4). The listed registration, recovery and export policies are acceptance targets for the relevant features; their presence here does not imply those routes already exist.

Every business API shall check a permission code and return HTTP 403 when the caller lacks access (REQ_NF422.5). Every create, update, delete, void, approval and posting shall be recorded in the audit log (REQ_NF422.6). Logs and API responses shall exclude passwords, tokens, full identity numbers and bank account numbers (REQ_NF422.8). Controller checks, integration tests and log review shall provide evidence for these requirements, including company and data-scope boundaries.

The dependency inventory shall contain zero packages with known high or critical vulnerabilities. The .NET and npm inventories shall be checked after every three completed modules (REQ_NF422.9). A finding requires remediation or a recorded decision before the security requirement can be accepted.
