The Inventory module (INV) records stock through an owner-controlled movement ledger. Its scope includes opening balances, other receipts and issues, transfers, stocktakes, assemblies and disassemblies, lot and serial tracking, safety-stock thresholds, valuation and movement reports. Each document separates draft or approval state from a posted movement. Quantity and value are calculated from committed movements; a posted movement is corrected through an explicit reversal rather than by changing a summary balance. These requirements are specified by REQ_F4115.

The module has implemented document workflows and three-language screens for several of these operations, including safety stock, valuation and movement reporting. The Inventory posting contract also accepts authorised external sources from purchasing and sales. Inventory remains in progress: outstanding work includes real browser and SQL verification through the normal development startup path and integration with unfinished purchasing and sales sources.

Company and data-scope permissions apply to document reads and mutations. Batch and export operations must report the actual committed result, including zero changes or skipped rows. Valuation distinguishes reliable cost from unpriced or anomalous stock; a quantity report must not be presented as a financial valuation.
