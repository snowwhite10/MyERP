The Supply Chain Management module (SCM) is planned to manage versioned demand forecasts, transport evidence and supplier collaboration under REQ_F41116. A forecast would retain its method, manual adjustments, approval and the committed source manifest used for each result. Transport events and delivery proof would be reconciled separately from PUR receipt, SD shipment and INV posting. Supplier proposals would be limited to authorised purchase orders and would not change PUR records until PUR confirms an outcome.

The first forecast design uses confirmed SD order demand as its required input; PP released demand and INV posted movements are separately labelled comparisons. MD conversion evidence and owner cutoffs must be replayable, and an unavailable required input prevents publication instead of becoming zero demand. SCM currently has requirements and refined source-contract designs only. No SCM reader, migration, service, page or operational forecast has been implemented or accepted.
