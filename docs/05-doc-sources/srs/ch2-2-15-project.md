The Project Management module (PS) is planned to maintain projects, approved budgets, work time, resource reservations, milestones and billable evidence under REQ_F41115. Historical state, company scope and an as-of cutoff would keep portfolio views reproducible. Approved time and accepted milestones would form a frozen billing source set so that each item is claimed at most once.

PS would verify employee identity and availability through HR, read posted actuals through FI and request invoicing through SD. An estimated project cost, billing submission or pending owner response would remain distinct from a confirmed posted cost, invoice or receivable. The PS baseline currently records requirements and owner contracts only. There are no PS migrations, endpoints, pages or accepted financial workflows.
