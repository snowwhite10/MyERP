This SRS is intended for the product owner and business reviewers who confirm scope and acceptance, analysts who maintain the requirement baseline, architects and developers who design and build the modules, and testers who verify the behavior. Operations staff can use the environment and quality sections when preparing a local installation or assessing support needs.

Readers seeking a product overview should begin with Chapters 1 and 2. Chapter 3 describes the analysis and feasibility basis. Implementers and testers should use Chapter 4 with the module requirements, business rules, API descriptions and test plans. The SDS describes the corresponding architecture and implemented database and interface design. The requirements workbook provides cross-document traceability; progress and backlog files identify work still open.
