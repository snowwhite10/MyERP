The Purchase module (PUR) covers purchase requisitions, requests for quotation, supplier quotations and comparison, purchase orders, receipts, returns and three-way invoice matching. A requisition records requested items and quantities; a comparison selects an evidenced supplier offer; an approved order controls receipt and return quantities. Inventory owns posted stock movements, while Finance owns the payable and general-ledger effects of a matched invoice. The detailed target criteria appear in REQ_F4114.

Purchase documents are separated into drafts, submitted and approved decisions, and any posted effects. Company, data-scope and action permissions protect reads and changes. Saved source identities, versions and quantities must survive retries and prevent a duplicate receipt, return or payable. A partially matched or rejected invoice requires a reason that can be traced to its lines.

The running PUR module includes an accepted requisition workflow and related three-language pages, plus implemented portions of the quotation workflow. Later purchase-order, receipt, return and three-way-match work units are unfinished or blocked pending real SQL verification. The design of those downstream functions is a requirement baseline, not evidence that the complete purchase-to-pay flow is available.
